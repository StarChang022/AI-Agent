### Financial Statements

- **Balance Sheet**: It analyzes the company's financial health. Think of it as a snapshot that shows exactly what a company owns and what it owes at one specific moment in time.
  - [Balance Sheet](statements/balance-sheet.html)
- **Income Statement**: This is basically the company's report card. It tells you how much money the company brought in (revenue) versus how much it spent (expenses) over a certain period, like a quarter or a year.
  - [Income Statement](statements/income-statement.html)
- **Cash Flow**: This shows you where the cash actually came from and where it actually went. It tracks the cold, hard cash the company put in its pocket, which isn't always the same as its 'profit' on the P&L.
  - [Cash Flow](statements/cash-flow.html)
