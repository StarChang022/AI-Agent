# Financial Ratios

## Decode Company Health in Minutes, Not Hours

You don't need a degree in accounting or advanced calculus to evaluate a stock. By mastering a few essential ratios, you can instantly cut through financial noise and see whether a business is truly profitable, deeply indebted, or generating real cash.

All it takes is simple math, business common sense, and the time it takes to finish a cup of coffee.
