# Privacy Policy


<!-- ----------- Content ----------- -->

Welcome to "ValueCafe" (hereinafter referred to as the "Site"). To help you feel secure while using the various services and information on this Site, we are explaining our Privacy Policy to protect your rights. Please read the following carefully:

## 1. Scope of the Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy covers how this Site processes personally identifiable information collected when you use our services. The Privacy Policy does not apply to related linked sites outside of this Site, nor does it apply to personnel not commissioned or involved in the management of this Site.

## 2. Collection, Processing, and Use of Personal Data

- When you visit this Site or use the functional services provided by this Site, we will ask you to provide necessary personal data depending on the nature of the service, and we will process and use your personal data within the scope of that specific purpose. Without your written consent, this Site will not use personal data for other purposes.
- When you use interactive features such as service emails and surveys, this Site will retain the name, email address, contact information, and time of use you provide.
- During normal browsing, the server will automatically record relevant behaviors, including the IP address of your connected device, time of use, browser used, and records of browsing and clicking data. This serves as a reference for us to improve our website services. This record is for internal use only and will never be published.
- To provide precise services, we will conduct statistics and analysis on the collected survey contents. The statistical data or explanatory text of the analysis results will be presented for internal research, and we may publish statistical data and explanatory text as needed, but it will not involve information of specific individuals.

## 3. Data Protection

- The hosts of this Site are equipped with firewalls, anti-virus systems, and other related information security devices and necessary security protection measures. Strict protection measures are adopted to protect the site and your personal data. Only authorized personnel can access your personal data. All relevant processing personnel have signed confidentiality agreements. Anyone who violates the confidentiality obligation will be subject to relevant legal actions.
- If it is necessary to entrust other units to provide services due to business needs, this Site will also strictly require them to comply with confidentiality obligations and take necessary inspection procedures to ensure their compliance.

## 4. External Links of the Website

The web pages of this Site provide internet links to other websites. You may also click to enter other websites through the links provided on this Site. However, the linked websites do not apply to the Privacy Policy of this Site. You must refer to the privacy policy of the linked websites.

## 5. Policy on Sharing Personal Data with Third Parties

This Site will never provide, exchange, rent, or sell any of your personal data to other individuals, groups, private companies, or public agencies, except when there is a legal basis or contractual obligation.

Exceptions to the above paragraph include but are not limited to:

- With your written consent.
- Expressly provided by law.
- To avoid danger to your life, body, freedom, or property.
- Cooperating with public agencies or academic research institutions for statistical or academic research based on public interest, and the data is processed or collected by the provider in a way that does not identify a specific party.
- When your behavior on the website violates the terms of service or may damage or hinder the rights of the website and other users or cause harm to anyone, the website management unit may disclose your personal data after analysis to identify, contact, or take legal action.
- Beneficial to your rights.
- When this Site entrusts vendors to assist in collecting, processing, or using your personal data, we will fulfill our responsibility to supervise and manage the outsourced vendors or individuals.

## 6. Use of Cookies

To provide you with the best service, this Site will place and access our Cookies on your computer. If you do not wish to accept the writing of Cookies, you can set the privacy level in your browser to high, which will refuse the writing of Cookies, but it may cause some functions of the website to not operate normally.

## 7. Amendments to the Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy of this Site will be revised as needed at any time. The revised terms will be published on the website.
