To Beginners

This homepage is your beginner's roadmap — we've laid out all the basics you need in logical order. Just scroll down to begin!

Welcome to the world of value investing!

You don't need to spend your days anxiously glued to the market tickers, and you don't have to lose sleep over it. Value investing is a much more calm and composed way to invest.

It’s about casually reading research over a cup of coffee, and only buying when the price is right.

This isn't just an investment strategy; it's practically a philosophy for life.

So, before we begin this journey, we're not going to jump straight into the techniques. Instead, we want to first share what's most important: the 'value investing worldview.

The Worldview of value investing

Balance Sheet

This is basically the company's report card. It tells you how much money the company brought in (revenue) versus how much it spent (expenses) over a certain period, like a quarter or a year.

Income Statement

This shows you where the cash actually came from and where it actually went. It tracks the cold, hard cash the company put in its pocket, which isn't always the same as its 'profit' on the P&L.
