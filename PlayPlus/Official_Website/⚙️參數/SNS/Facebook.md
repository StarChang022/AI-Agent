# Web Design Davao

## 1

Does a button tell people what it will do? “View services” gives more context than “Click here” when that is where the link leads. Read your button labels alongside their destinations. Which one could be clearer?

## 2

Open a page of your website and read a full paragraph on your phone. Does it feel comfortable, or are you squinting and zooming? Text size, spacing and contrast all deserve a look. Save this as a practical reading check.

## 3

What should a new customer bring to a first conversation? A short description of the need, relevant details and any questions are a useful start. Add a simple preparation note to your website. Save this idea for your next update.

## 2

把一隻烏鴉變成 ASCII 字元風格，再融進紅黑海報設計裏。🐦‍⬛

這次用 Adobe Firefly，將烏鴉參考圖轉成白色字母、數字與符號組成的圖像，再回到 Photoshop 完成合成。

本次選用 Gemini 3.1（Nano Banana 2），解析度設為 4K。下載圖片後，抓住這三個操作：
① 混合模式改成「濾色」，讓黑底不再蓋住海報。
② 縮放、移動，對齊原本的烏鴉輪廓。
③ 新增圖層遮色片，用黑色筆刷藏起壓到矩形框線的字元。

生成時先看烏鴉輪廓有沒有保留，合成後再檢查框線與主體交界，讓密集的字元也有清楚的層次。

提示詞直接複製：
烏鴉造型的ASCII ART風格，單純由白色字母、數字、符號字元組成，字元不重疊，不疊加陰影，背景黑色
這支示範著重字元圖像的生成與合成，搭配已事先完成版面的概念素材。
